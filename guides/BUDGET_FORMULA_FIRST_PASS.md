# Budget formula first pass, 2026-11-07

The supplied formula document is an unverified research lead, not confirmed
game source; the user clarified it was generated by another LLM. The first calculator pass implements player income, research,
intelligence, academy costs and dock expansion. A second pass adds aircraft,
in-service submarine maintenance, active submarine construction, base expansion,
invasion, and the peace-era maintenance multiplier. A third pass implements player
ship and installation maintenance, all-active maintenance, and training priorities.
The monthly balance range remains while untested cases are investigated.

## Comparison with saved campaigns

Using the installed `Data/MapData.dat` and current saved maps, yearly income
and research matched the recorded screenshot for each of Games 1–6, 8 and 9.
The USA 1971 example yields 1,949,230 yearly, 162,436 monthly and 16,244
research. Game 7 was replaced with a Game 6 copy and cannot be compared to its
older screenshot. Spain Game 8 yields intelligence 60 and academy 10; its
dock expansion calculation is 324. These comparisons do not prove rules that
were not exercised, including AI-nation budget differences and HFR research.

The game installation map and a campaign map can have different possession
slot numbers. The implementation matches the area and possession name when
the original slots do not match; ambiguous or unavailable map data falls back
to the old income estimate and marks the income labels as fallback.

## Third-pass comparison

Games 1–6, 8 and 9 now reproduce the displayed yearly and monthly budget,
maintenance, all-active reference, construction, aircraft, research, training,
intelligence, total and balance in their available screenshots. Game3 uses
the later 6,454 construction screenshot and Game6 uses the November 1971
screenshot. Game9's remaining 36 was explained by applying the foreign-airbase
multiplier to four airship bases. Game3's training excludes 21 of ship
maintenance because that ship is under repair. The all-active reference uses
all-active surface costs while preserving ordinary installation costs; skipping
the installation modifier did not match the screenshots.

The original BCS and map hashes still match for Games 1, 2, 4, 5 and 9. Games 3,
6 and 8 have changed since the historical snapshot, and matching visible fields
does not prove every input matches. Officer-file hashes were not recorded in the
original observations. Older KE obsolete dates appear to use a 1900-based day
count; its exact encoding is unverified, and dates near the inferred boundary
fall back to the earlier maintenance estimate.
